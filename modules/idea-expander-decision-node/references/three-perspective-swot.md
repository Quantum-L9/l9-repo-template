# Three-Perspective SWOT Protocol

Run three independent SWOT analyses before synthesis.

## Perspective A: Autonomous Deployability with AI Agents and L9

Evaluate:

- decomposition into bounded agent jobs
- deterministic contracts and schemas
- tool and connector availability
- orchestration and state ownership
- evidence, audit, and observability
- CI/CD, test, release, rollback, and recovery
- human approval boundaries
- security, privacy, and protected actions
- corpus and knowledge dependencies
- self-improvement controls
- cost, latency, and operational complexity
- fit with reusable L9 constellation capabilities

Required output:

- deployable now
- deployable with conditions
- not autonomously deployable yet
- required constellation nodes
- missing capabilities
- human-in-the-loop points
- proof gates

## Perspective B: Marketability and Saleability

Evaluate:

- painful and frequent customer problem
- precise ideal customer profile
- compelling value proposition
- category clarity and message simplicity
- trust burden and credibility requirements
- buyer, user, and budget-owner alignment
- sales cycle and procurement friction
- acquisition channels and distribution leverage
- offer design and pricing comprehension
- proof, case study, and demonstration needs
- retention, expansion, and referral potential
- competitive substitutes and switching costs

Required output:

- demand thesis
- saleability blockers
- fastest validation experiments
- minimum credible offer
- channel strategy
- proof assets required

## Perspective C: Profitability and Economic Resilience

Evaluate:

- revenue model
- price and willingness-to-pay assumptions
- direct and indirect costs
- contribution margin
- customer acquisition cost
- payback period
- lifetime value
- operating leverage
- working capital and cash timing
- capital intensity
- downside exposure
- scalability constraints
- concentration risk
- defensibility and compounding assets

Required output:

- base, downside, and upside economic thesis
- break-even drivers
- margin risks
- capital requirements
- sensitivity variables
- path to durable profit

## Scoring

Score each perspective from 1 to 5 on:

- evidence strength
- feasibility
- leverage
- resilience
- repairability

Also identify fatal blockers separately. Never average away a fatal blocker.

## Synthesis

Produce:

- shared strengths
- cross-perspective contradictions
- compounding advantages
- coupled risks
- highest-leverage modification
- evidence acquisition priorities
