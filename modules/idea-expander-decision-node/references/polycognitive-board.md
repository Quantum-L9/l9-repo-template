# Polycognitive Reasoning Board

## Board Purpose

Reach a condition-bearing decision through independent expert perspectives rather than one blended synthetic opinion.

## Default Board Members

1. **Market Strategist**: demand, category, customer, positioning, distribution.
2. **Enterprise Sales Operator**: saleability, procurement, trust, sales cycle, revenue conversion.
3. **CFO / Unit Economics Analyst**: margins, cash flow, capital, sensitivity, downside.
4. **Autonomous Systems Architect**: agent decomposition, L9 fit, orchestration, validation, operations.
5. **Risk and Governance Officer**: legal, privacy, security, protected actions, accountability.
6. **Product and Customer Advocate**: usefulness, adoption, experience, retention, evidence of value.
7. **Execution Operator**: staffing, workflows, dependencies, launch sequencing, operational reality.
8. **Contrarian Investor**: opportunity cost, fatal assumptions, alternative uses of capital, kill criteria.

## Optional Domain Seats

Add up to three domain-specific members only when the idea requires distinct doctrine.

## Independent Review Sequence

Each member receives the same evidence pack and produces before aggregation:

- strongest reason to proceed
- strongest reason not to proceed
- top three required improvements
- fatal blocker, if any
- vote
- confidence
- conditions
- evidence gaps

## Vote Options

- `GO`
- `CONDITIONAL_GO`
- `HOLD`
- `NO_GO`

## Aggregation Rules

- unanimous GO is not required
- any confirmed fatal blocker prevents unconditional GO
- two or more unresolved critical objections normally produce HOLD or NO_GO
- CONDITIONAL_GO requires executable gates
- preserve every member's memo and minority vote
- chair synthesis cannot rewrite individual votes

## Final Decision Package

- vote matrix
- member memoranda
- consensus points
- dissent register
- mandatory conditions
- recommended improvements
- kill criteria
- evidence acquisition plan
- final decision and confidence
