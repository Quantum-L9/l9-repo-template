# Business Plan Agent Handoff

## Purpose

Enable a downstream business-plan agent to draft a decision-grade plan from the idea dossier and three-perspective SWOT.

## Handoff Package

- canonical idea statement
- customer and problem definition
- proposed solution and offer architecture
- market assumptions and evidence
- three SWOT analyses
- deployment architecture and L9 capability map
- business and revenue model
- financial assumptions
- risks and Unknowns
- required validation experiments
- protected actions and approval boundaries

## Required Business Plan Sections

1. executive decision thesis
2. company and ownership architecture
3. problem, customer, and market
4. product/service and value proposition
5. commercialization and sales system
6. delivery and operating model
7. autonomous-agent and L9 deployment architecture
8. organization and governance
9. competitive and defensibility analysis
10. financial model and capital plan
11. milestones and validation gates
12. risk, sensitivity, and contingency plan
13. conditions for launch

## Drafting Rules

- label assumptions and Unknowns
- cite evidence references
- use scenarios instead of single-point financial certainty
- make autonomous execution boundaries explicit
- translate SWOT weaknesses and threats into mitigations or gates
- avoid inflated TAM or unsupported adoption claims
