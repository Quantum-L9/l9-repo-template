# Expansion Protocol

## 1. Original center

Record without improvement:
- Dream
- Invariant
- current Wedge
- current Proof
- target beneficiary/customer
- economic thesis
- explicit constraints
- anti-goals

## 2. Wide discovery

Explore only branches that could change value or execution probability:
- adjacent buyers/customers;
- adjacent use cases;
- alternative wedges;
- strategic partners;
- channels/distribution;
- proof environments;
- business models;
- geographies/regulatory arbitrage where relevant;
- acquisition/exit paths where relevant.

## 3. Deep discovery

Explore:
- reusable capability/substrate;
- proprietary evidence or data exhaust;
- learning loops;
- network effects;
- switching cost/embeddedness;
- internal platform value;
- ecosystem leverage;
- standards or interoperability leverage;
- defensibility;
- future-option creation;
- second-order access or distribution effects.

## 4. Independent lenses

Select 2-4 lenses capable of changing the idea. Typical lenses:
- customer/buyer;
- founder/operator;
- investor/capital allocator;
- distribution/growth;
- domain expert;
- systems architect;
- regulator/legal;
- competitor;
- skeptic/auditor.

For each lens record:
- observation;
- decision effect;
- concrete modification;
- evidence state.

Delete lenses that produce no decision effect.

## 5. Compounding loops

A valid loop must identify:
- trigger/action;
- accumulating asset;
- why the next cycle improves;
- limiting factor;
- proof that would validate the loop.

Do not label a one-way funnel a flywheel.

## 6. Candidate convergence

Each candidate receives exactly one disposition:

- `integrate_now`: materially improves the current center/wedge and is supported enough to act on now.
- `sequence_later`: valuable after a named dependency or proof gate.
- `preserve_option`: strategically valuable but premature; retain without adding current scope.
- `discard`: weak, distracting, contradictory, or inferior.

## 7. Revised center

The final center must state:
- revised Dream;
- locked Invariant;
- revised Wedge;
- revised Proof;
- what changed;
- what remained unchanged;
- what is deliberately not being built now.
