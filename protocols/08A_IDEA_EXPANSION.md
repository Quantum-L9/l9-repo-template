# 08A - Idea Expansion Stage

`idea-expander` is the mandatory upstream semantic-development module whenever routing requires expansion or the user explicitly asks to develop, deepen, broaden, harden, or turn a spark into a full idea dossier.

## Stage contract

```text
CENTER_RESOLVED
   -> idea-expander
   -> expansion_packet.json
   -> deterministic expansion gate
   -> EXPANDED_READY | EXPANSION_BLOCKED
   -> idea-expander-decision-node when decision adjudication is required
```

## Ownership

The model/skill owns creative expansion and revised-center judgment.
The deterministic gate owns structural integrity, coverage, authority separation, and handoff readiness.
The decision node owns GO / CONDITIONAL_GO / HOLD / NO_GO.

## Fail-closed rules

Expansion is blocked when:
- Dream / Invariant / Wedge / Proof is absent;
- no material wide/deep discovery exists;
- a discovery lacks exactly one disposition;
- the expander hides a material Unknown;
- the revised center absorbs every attractive branch without prioritization;
- the expander emits a final decision reserved for the decision node.
