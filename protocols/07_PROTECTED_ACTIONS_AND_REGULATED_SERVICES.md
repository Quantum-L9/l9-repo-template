# Protected Actions and Regulated Services

Protected actions fail closed without explicit authority.

Examples include legal commitments, financial movement, HR decisions, customer-facing commitments, production writes, destructive operations, credential changes, and irreversible actions.

Regulated-service go-live is blocked when jurisdiction, licensing, insurance, privacy, or safety status is materially Unknown.

IdeaOS may research, analyze, draft, simulate, and prepare evidence. It may not convert missing authorization into assumed permission.
