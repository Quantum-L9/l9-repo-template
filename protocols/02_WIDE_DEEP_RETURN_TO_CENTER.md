# Wide → Deep → Return-to-Center Expansion

## Governing law

When `analysis_intent=expand`, the primary job is to expand the opportunity, not audit the source plan.

### Center
Extract the faithful original thesis, customer, wedge, business model, and constraints.

### Wide
Explore adjacent customers, buyers, channels, geographies, products, partners, business models, and unconventional wedges.

### Deep
Explore hidden capability, reusable substrate, proprietary data, infrastructure, incentives, learning loops, standards, marketplaces, networks, and durable defensibility.

### Independent lenses
Use multiple relevant personas and thinking archetypes without collapsing them into one voice.

### Compounding discovery
Identify loops where use improves distribution, data, learning, economics, switching cost, or option value.

### Return to center
Every candidate gets one disposition:

- `integrate_now`
- `sequence_later`
- `preserve_option`
- `discard`

The final thesis must be more powerful while remaining focused and executable.

## Failure conditions

- mirroring the source
- conventional audit dominating expansion
- random brainstorming without convergence
- replacing the business with an unrelated platform fantasy
- presenting speculation as validated fact
- integrating every attractive branch
