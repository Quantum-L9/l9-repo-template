# Dream → Invariant → Wedge → Proof

Every developed idea must resolve four layers before execution promotion.

## Dream
What future state is worth creating?

## Invariant
What must remain true for the idea to still be this idea?

## Wedge
What is the smallest credible entry point that proves demand or capability without prematurely building the whole vision?

## Proof
What observable test would materially increase or decrease confidence?

A stronger vision does not excuse a weak wedge. A clever wedge does not excuse a missing invariant.
