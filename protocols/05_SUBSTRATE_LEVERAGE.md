# Substrate Leverage Protocol

Substrate leverage is a first-class decision dimension, not a boolean footnote.

Score 0–5 on:

- reuse frequency
- workflows improved
- recurring friction removed
- future option value
- switching cost / embeddedness created
- downstream acceleration

A foundational substrate score forces explicit comparison against the direct commercial wedge.

The output must explain the compounding mechanism and identify the smallest safe pilot. Do not let infrastructure ambition become an excuse to avoid proving value.
