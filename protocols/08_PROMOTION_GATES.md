# Promotion Gates

A deep idea may become `execution_ready` only when:

- Dream, Invariant, Wedge, and Proof exist
- route is resolved
- material Unknowns are bounded
- material contradictions are handled
- required research/coding/runtime/protected-action contracts exist
- regulated-service gates exist when applicable
- source lineage is accounted for when multi-source
- downstream execution can begin without basic rediscovery

Promotion is evaluated by `runtime/promotion.py` and `policies/promotion.yaml`.
