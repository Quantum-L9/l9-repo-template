# Operating Model

IdeaOS is a governed reasoning operating system, not one giant agent and not a rigid workflow engine.

## Five layers

1. **Doctrine** - human-readable laws for idea development.
2. **Cognitive modules** - bounded skills such as `idea-expander` and the decision node.
3. **Contracts** - machine-readable expansion, decision, promotion, return, and execution artifacts.
4. **Runtime** - deterministic ingress, gating, routing, promotion, and reintegration.
5. **Evaluation and learning** - benchmarks and reviewable learning candidates.

## Authority split

```text
Creative / semantic judgment        Deterministic enforcement
-----------------------------       -------------------------
Dream                               classification validity
Invariant                           route consequences
Wedge                               expansion handoff integrity
Proof                               required artifacts
Wide/deep discovery                 source/provenance identity
Return-to-Center synthesis          promotion gates
Persona/archetype effects           protected-action gates
Substrate judgment                  return-envelope validity
Meaning of contradictions           benchmark scoring
                                    learning-patch eligibility
```

The decision node is neither the creative expander nor the executor. It is the adjudication boundary between a developed idea and execution authorization.
